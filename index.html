How it works
• Board state → 12×8×8 bit-planes (piece type & color) → 768-dim vector
• Value network estimates how good a position is for the AI (−1…1).
• After each game, all AI positions are stored with the final outcome (win/lose/draw).
• Every 16 games, the model trains on this replay buffer and is saved to Firestore at artifacts/neuralzero-chess/public/data/hive_memory/model and cached in localStorage.
• The AI uses an ε-greedy one-ply search (look ahead one move) to choose moves.
